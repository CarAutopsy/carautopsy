## Rules for closing a gap

- Never invent a price, specification, VIN or identity field.
- Never treat a dealer asking price or used-car price as new-car MSRP.
- Never convert a foreign-market price into an Austrian price without recording
  source market, currency, FX date, FX rate and adjustment method.
- Never promote an approximate, cross-market or range candidate to `verified`
  without a new owner-reviewed correction.
- Record source URL, source date, VAT basis, confidence and `review_required: true`
  for every accepted replacement price.
