**Status:** planning only. This planning document performs no retrieval, assigns no price, and creates no staging or catalog record. The companion source-leads register separately documents previously completed bounded website consultations on 2026-11-22; those consultations are not evidence of verified Austrian MSRP, authorized provider use, or catalog eligibility.

`docs/research/pilot-gap-price-candidates.json` into three owner-controlled research waves.

## Wave definitions

- **Wave 1** — highest Austrian market value: Tesla Model 3/Y and the named BYD models.
- **Wave 2** — high-volume mainstream gaps from the Batch 001 scope.
- **Wave 3** — non-Austrian, no-credible-pathway or owner-decision cases.

Wave counts: Wave 1 = 13 (9 price gaps + 4 approximate verification-only XPeng rows), Wave 2 = 22 (18 price gaps + 4 approximate verification-only VW rows), Wave 3 = 9. The owner-named VW and XPeng models already have approximate prices, so they are included for provenance review rather than price research.
